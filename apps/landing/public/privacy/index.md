# Privacy

> Built for teams, not for watching people. Standcast suggests blockers from your own signals. Those suggestions belong to you. A test in CI checks that they never leave your draft.

## Who sees what

Your team sees:

- Dailies you confirmed, in the team digest
- Blockers you confirmed, routed to who can help
- Who hasn't sent today's daily

Only you see:

- A pull request waiting days for review
- CI failing on your branch for a day
- A Linear card stuck in progress

No productivity metrics. No rankings. No commit counts. Nothing sent on anyone's behalf.

## What Standcast reads

Only what a daily needs. What Standcast reads, what it stores, how long it keeps it and who sees what are all in the [privacy page of the documentation](https://docs-standcast.usefachada.dev/privacy).

- **No AI on our servers.** No LLM processes your data on the Standcast servers. The draft comes from your GitHub and Linear activity, and the digest from what people confirmed.
- **Your code stays yours.** The GitHub App reads pull requests, reviews, checks, commit metadata, standcast.yml and CODEOWNERS. It never reads source files and never writes to your repositories.
- **Suggestions stay in your draft.** Suggested blockers are visible only to you. They never reach the digest, the API, webhooks or managers. Managers read confirmed content only.
