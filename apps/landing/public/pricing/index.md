# Pricing

> Free during the public beta. Standcast is free while it is in public beta. Pricing will be announced before the beta ends.

## What that means

- **Free during the beta.** Teams in the public beta use Standcast for free.
- **Announced before the beta ends.** Pricing will be announced before the public beta ends.
- **Open source.** Standcast is MIT licensed. The [self-hosting section of the README](https://github.com/ImFelipeOliveira/standcast#self-hosting) covers the API, the worker and Postgres.
